ASEAN Digital Health & Climate Resilience Initiative (ADHCRI)

Scenario
The ASEAN Regional Health Resilience Programme (ARHRP) proposed ADHCRI to strengthen how health systems across Southeast Asia detect, report and respond to public health threats. Public health information was being collected through disconnected systems, spreadsheets, email, telephone and paper reporting, causing delays, duplicate entry, incomplete reports and limited visibility of emerging disease patterns.
My role
Health Business Analyst within the consulting team engaged by ARHRP.
Scope
  • Stakeholder analysis, stakeholder matrices and a RACI
  • SWOT, PESTLE, McKinsey 7S, Fishbone and 5 Whys analyses
  • Country readiness assessment and current-state documentation
  • AS-IS and TO-BE process maps with a structured gap analysis
  • Five-year business case and financial analysis
  • Business Requirements Document, risk assessment and implementation recommendations
  • Jira epics, user stories, acceptance criteria and at least three sprints
Tools
Jira, draw.io, Microsoft Word / Google Docs

Closed-Loop Medication & Pharmacy Optimisation at Apex Health Network

Scenario
Apex Health Network runs 12 inpatient hospitals and 45 outpatient clinics. Prescribing, pharmacy inventory (on legacy standalone software) and bedside administration (paper charts and verbal confirmation) were disconnected. The case describes medication errors and adverse drug events, no real-time inventory visibility, stockouts of critical drugs, financial leakage from expired or unbilled medication, nurses spending up to 30% of a shift double-checking paper orders, controlled-substance compliance risk and limited analytics.
My role
Business Analyst on a clinical operations and pharmacy technology initiative.
Scope
  • Identification of at least 10 project stakeholders, and an approach to identify, prioritise, engage and communicate with 100+ stakeholders
  • Stakeholder Analysis Matrix, RACI Matrix (10+ activities) and Stakeholder Communication Plan
  • Business and clinical problem analysis
  • Recommended digital solution for closed-loop medication management
  • Business case for the Project Sponsor
  • SDLC methodology recommendation with justification
Tools
Stakeholder, RACI, communication plan and business case templates

Drug Delivery Process Improvement at MedBridge Pharmacy

Scenario
MedBridge Pharmacy offers routine home delivery, but delivery took up to 7 days against a 3-day target. Capture errors affected 12% of orders and staff could see the status of only 40% of them. Separate order and stock records, calls and messages meant patients kept phoning for updates while staff spent time locating orders and correcting information.
My role
Healthtech Business Analyst: gather evidence, engage stakeholders, map workflows and document requirements.
Scope
  • Gap analysis comparing current and desired states, with root causes, impact and action plans, plus a Fishbone diagram
  • AS-IS and TO-BE process maps built in draw.io
  • Business Requirements Document covering business overview, objectives, scope, stakeholders and proposed solution
  • Business, stakeholder, functional and non-functional requirements
  • MoSCoW prioritisation (Must, Should, Could, Won't Have for Now)
Tools
Gap analysis template, draw.io, BRD

Scenario
VitaCore Supplements Ltd. sells multiple supplement products across countries, regions, cities, sales channels and sales representatives. Management lacked a clear view of what was driving revenue, profit, product returns and customer satisfaction, including products with high sales but weak profit.

Healthcare Data Analyst responsible for cleaning and analysing the sales dataset in Power BI.

  • Calculated columns and measures for revenue, profit margin, return rate and average rating
  • Performance analysis by product, sales rep, channel, country, city and region

  • Recommendations to improve sales, profit, returns and customer satisfaction

Power BI

Immunisation Programme Analysis Across Asia

Scenario
Governments across Asia invest heavily in immunisation programmes, and public health officials need reliable data to see which vaccines are administered most, which countries have the highest activity and where more support may be needed. The Ministry of Health asked five questions about vaccines, countries, regions above 500,000 doses, the least administered vaccine and the highest average vaccinations per report.
My role
Healthcare Data Analyst using SQL to query the immunisation database.
Scope
  • Reviewing the countries and immunization_records tables
  • Filtering with WHERE by country, region, vaccine and report year
  • Joining records to countries on country_id
  • Aggregating with SUM, COUNT, AVG, GROUP BY and HAVING
  • Ranking results with ORDER BY to answer the five business questions
Tools
PostgreSQL

Patient Appointment No-Show Analysis at TrueNorth Health Network

Scenario
TrueNorth Health Network operates clinics across Europe and North America. Appointment no-shows waste doctor time, reduce clinic revenue and delay care for other patients, and leadership needed to know which locations, patient groups and scheduling patterns carry the highest no-show risk.
My role
Healthcare Data Analyst cleaning and analysing appointment data in Excel.
Scope
  • Cleaning duplicates, missing values, inconsistent text, invalid dates, wrong ages and mixed no-show labels
  • Pivot tables, charts and formulas by country, clinic branch, specialty, age group, reminder channel and wait days
  • Analysis of previous no-show history and scheduling gaps
  • Excel dashboard with KPIs, trends, pivot charts and slicers
  • Recommendations on where management should act first
Tools
Microsoft Excel
